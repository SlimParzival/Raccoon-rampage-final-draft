RACCOON RAMPAGE v0.7 — ART ATTACK

This build pushes the visual identity much harder:
- Comic-book menu presentation
- Illustrated city/dump/rooftop scenery
- More detailed custom-drawn raccoon with skin-specific accessories
- Stronger enemy silhouettes and health bars

- Bosses get large illustrated silhouettes and clearer presentation
- Retains v0.6 gameplay tuning: smoother joystick, easier opening difficulty, stronger attacks, better coin progression
- Retains v0.5 systems: sound, daily challenge, weapon evolutions, Chef Crow, Mutant Opossum, Rival Raccoon, Garbage King, achievements and permanent upgrades

This is still the browser prototype. The next major production art step would be original sprite sheets/animations and full illustrated asset packs.
